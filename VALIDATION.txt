VALIDATION

8 automated checks passed in the creation environment (Linux):
- Every format/resolution combination parses with the installed yt-dlp.
- Source URL validation and domain matching.
- Best-available and capped resolution selection.
- Silent videos are supported.
- Real local downloads/conversions to MP4, MKV, WebM and AVI.
  All four retained 600 x 600 dimensions and audio.
- Structured progress updates and final output paths.
- Cancellation of a running download subprocess.
- Cancellation before the subprocess starts.

The GUI imports in about 0.05 seconds here, without importing yt-dlp or
contacting a website. This is not a measurement of Windows window startup.
Windows setup, live hosting-site downloads and GUI layout could not be
verified in this environment. The under-three-second startup target
requires a check on your Windows 11 PC.
